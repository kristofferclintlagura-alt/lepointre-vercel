<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-family:Arial,Helvetica,sans-serif;"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="color: rgb(0, 128, 0);">De 1985 &agrave; 1991, Le Pointre a r&eacute;alis&eacute; plus de 300 toiles de grand format qu&#39;il a placard&eacute;es sur les murs de New York. Today those works stay alive only through these pictures.</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></p>
