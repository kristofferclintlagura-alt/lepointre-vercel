<p><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span><span><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Comme un cri sourd qui se laisse &agrave; voir ! Masqu&eacute; d&#39;un sourire, ironie du pire, politesse du d&eacute;sespoir.</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span><span><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Larmes d&#39;yeux qui collent sur la toile, Oedipe d&eacute;chu</span></span></span></span></span> <span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">pleurant des larmes de sang color&eacute;es, iris crev&eacute;s.</span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Univers impalpable, visions redondantes et obs&eacute;dantes de l&#39;enfant pris dans la trame tragi-comique de la toile de la condition humaine :&nbsp; clown triste ! Mouchet&eacute;es</span></span></span><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">, &eacute;claboussures impulsives, la peinture brute et brutale : brutalit&eacute; ind&eacute;cente d&#39;un pass&eacute; volcanique jaillissant au pr&eacute;sent.</span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Violence des couleurs, toiles g&eacute;antes pour un homme qui se voit grand ?</span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Humanit&eacute; d&eacute;chir&eacute;e, masques nus port&eacute;s aux nues de la douleur de vivre. Angoissants personnages que l&#39;&acirc;me ne peut d&eacute;crire. Violence de l&#39;&acirc;me, impulsion impudique, d&eacute;chirure b&eacute;ante d&#39;un univers tant&ocirc;t chaotique, tant&ocirc;t candide, onirique. Spasmes d&eacute;rangeants.</span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Na&iuml;vet&eacute; de l&#39;enfance d&eacute;chir&eacute;e sur des rives acerbes. Jets explosifs de couleurs sur les toiles qui ne sont pas sans taches. Force &eacute;ruptive de la lave inconsciente se donnant &agrave; cr&eacute;er. Jets chaotiques d&#39;un univers myst&eacute;rieux.</span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Like a muffled cry that has taken flesh visually. Hidden by a smile, betraying both the irony ans politeness of someonefaced with despair.</span></span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p><span"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Tears shed by the eyes that stick to the canvas, the fallen OEdipus&#39;s bright blood tears that flow from his slashed irises.</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p><span"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">A hidden, intangible universe ; repeated and obsessive visions of a child caught up in the tragi-comic web of the human condition : a sad clown. Slappered and dabbled paint, brutal raw painting - the indecent brutality of a volcanic past erupting here and now.</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">The clash of colours with giant canvases for a man who believes himself to be great ?</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Humanity torn apart, naked masks showing the pain of living bare. Acutely distressing characters that the soul can&#39;t describe.Inner-felt violence, immodest impulse, gaping wound in a universe that can be chaotic, candidly harmless, and dreamlike in turn. Disturbing spasms.</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p><span"><span class="yui3-widget yui3-inputwidget yui3-htmleditorwidget" style="width: 638px;"><span class="yui3-htmleditorwidget-content"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-family: georgia,serif;">Childhood naivety torn away on bitter shores. Explosive spurts of color on the canvases that are not devoid of blotches - the chaotic spurts of a mysterious universe.</span></span></span></span></span></p>
